Types of golf cart batteries

Deep cycle (lead-acid)

Built for repeated deep discharge and charging, the common choice for golf carts.

Lithium

A modern option for steady performance and longer life, and we confirm compatibility with your cart.

Voltage and wiring

A cart's battery pack is made of several batteries (6, 8 or 12 volt) wired together to give the system voltage.

How we choose the right battery for your cart

Carts differ in system voltage, number of batteries and wiring, so we start with the basics and then recommend an option.

FAQ

How does deep cycle differ from a normal car battery?

A deep cycle battery is built for repeated deep discharge, while a car battery delivers a strong starting current for a short time. One cannot replace the other.

How many batteries does a golf cart need?

It depends on the cart's system voltage; some carts use several batteries wired together. Send us a photo of your batteries and we will confirm the number and type.

Can I switch to lithium?

In some carts yes, but voltage and charger must match. Contact us and we confirm before you buy.
