Differences between car battery types

Standard battery

The traditional choice for most cars without a Start-Stop system.

EFB battery

Built to withstand the frequent restarts of cars with a light Start-Stop system.

AGM battery

The highest endurance, usually required by European and modern cars with advanced electrics.

How to choose your car battery

The right decision starts with three points: the size that fits your battery tray, the type your car requires, and the brand that suits your use.

  • Find the size with the battery finder by make, model and year.
  • If your car is a recent European or has Start-Stop, choose EFB or AGM.
  • Compare the available brands: VARTA, BOSCH, ACDelco, AMARON, GENESIS and more.

FAQ

What is the difference between AGM and EFB?

EFB suits cars with a light Start-Stop system, while AGM offers the highest endurance and is usually required by European and modern cars. We confirm the right one for your car.

Can I fit AGM in place of a standard battery?

Usually yes in terms of size, but it is best to confirm compatibility with your car first. Contact us.

How long does a car battery last?

It varies with use, heat and alternator health. We recommend periodic testing, especially after 3 to 4 years.
