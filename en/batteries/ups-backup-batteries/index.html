Types of backup power batteries

Sealed batteries (SLA / AGM)

Common in UPS units, alarm systems and emergency lighting, with no routine maintenance.

Gel batteries

Suited to deep discharge and applications needing frequent charge cycles.

Lithium batteries

A modern lightweight option for systems that need a longer life, on request and subject to availability.

How to choose a UPS or device battery

The right battery is set by the device voltage, its capacity in amp-hours (Ah), its dimensions and the terminal position. Common capacities in small devices are 7, 9 and 12 Ah at 12 volts, and larger systems use capacities such as 100 and 200 Ah (subject to availability).

  • Read the voltage and capacity printed on your current battery.
  • Measure the dimensions and note the terminal position.
  • Send us a photo of the battery or the UPS model.
  • For projects and larger quantities we quote on request.

FAQ

How do I find the right UPS battery?

From the current battery label: voltage and capacity (Ah). Send us a photo or the UPS model and we confirm the replacement.

Do UPS batteries need replacing periodically?

Yes, battery life is limited and varies with use and heat. When runtime drops or the unit shows an alert, have the battery tested and replaced.
