How to choose a heavy vehicle battery

Some vehicles run at 12 volts and others at 24 volts using two batteries together, so we confirm the size and quantity before supply.

  • Tell us the truck or machine type and year.
  • Send a photo of the current battery or its data plate if possible.
  • State the quantity if you are buying for a fleet.
  • We arrange supply and delivery to your site in the Eastern Province, or shipping to other cities.
