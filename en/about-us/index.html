About us: Arkan Energy Trading

Arkan Energy Trading was founded in 2017 in Saudi Arabia as a national company specialising in vehicle power solutions. A team that cares about keeping you moving and your vehicle safe.

Call now 0555082350WhatsApp

Our story

We started in 2017 with a clear vision: to give drivers and companies a reliable battery and honest advice. We have grown to 8 branches in Dammam, Khobar and Jubail, a mobile fitting service in the Eastern Province, and shipping to cities across the Kingdom. We are proud to be the exclusive agent for SHUBA lithium batteries in the Eastern Province.

Our vision

To be a leading partner in vehicle power, taking the sector toward new levels of excellence in line with Saudi Vision 2030, as a model for quality, reliability and innovation.

Our mission

To be the right choice for every customer seeking quality and performance, by providing highly efficient products and services and contributing to the national economy.
