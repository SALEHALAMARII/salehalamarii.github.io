Battery size and type FAQ

How do I find my car battery size?

The easiest way is to choose your make, model and year in the tool above. You can also read the code printed on your current battery (for example 55D23L), check the owner's manual, or visit your nearest branch and we will confirm it.

What does a battery code like 55D23L mean?

The first number indicates capacity, the letter and number after it (D23) indicate the case size and approximate length, and the final L or R shows the positive terminal position (left or right). The size must match your battery tray and terminal layout.

What is the difference between standard, EFB and AGM batteries?

Standard batteries suit most conventional cars. EFB is stronger and copes with the frequent restarts of light Start-Stop cars. AGM offers the highest endurance and is typically used in European cars and vehicles with advanced electrics. Replace with the type your car needs.

Does my car need an AGM battery?

Usually yes if it is a recent European car or has an automatic Start-Stop system. If you are not sure, contact us and we will confirm the right type before you buy.

What are the signs my battery needs replacing?

Slow cranking, the car not starting first time, dimming dashboard lights, a battery warning light, or a battery older than 3 to 4 years. A digital test confirms its condition.

Can the battery be fitted at my location?

Yes within the Eastern Province (Dammam, Khobar, Jubail, Dhahran, Qatif and Saihat) during service hours: Sat–Thu 7:30 AM – 11 PM, Friday 4 PM – 11 PM. Other Saudi cities are served by shipping from our store.

What about hybrid and electric cars?

They contain an auxiliary 12V battery with different specifications from conventional cars, so please contact us to confirm the right size for your vehicle.
