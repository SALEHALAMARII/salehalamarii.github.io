How to choose the right branch in Dammam

Four of our branches sit close together in Al Fayha on Omar Bin Al Khattab Street, known as High Pressure Street, and are easy to tell apart by nearby landmarks: one opposite Lian Hyper, one opposite Al Qowa Hall, and one beside the King Saud Road intersection signal next to Al Rajhi Remittance. If you are on the other side of the city, your nearest branch is on King Fahd Road after Othaim Markets and before the Othman Bin Affan intersection, next to DHL.

What you find at our Dammam branches

  • Car batteries: Standard, EFB and AGM for many makes and models.
  • SHUBA lithium: Exclusive agent for SHUBA lithium in the Eastern Province.
  • Trucks and equipment: Heavy duty batteries for trucks and equipment.
  • Golf, UPS and motorcycles: Golf cart, device and motorcycle batteries.
