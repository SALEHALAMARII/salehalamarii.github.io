FAQ

How many Arkan Energy branches are there?

We have 8 branches: five in Dammam (four in Al Fayha and one on King Fahd Road), two in Khobar (Al Thuqba and South Al Rakah), and one in Jubail Industrial City (Al Musanad).

Where is the main branch?

The main branch is in Dammam, Al Fayha, on Omar Bin Al Khattab Street (High Pressure Street), opposite Lian Hyper.

Do you come to me if I am far from a branch?

Yes within the Eastern Province: Dammam, Khobar, Dhahran, Jubail, Qatif and Saihat. A technician fits the battery at your location during service hours. Details are on the delivery and fitting page.

What if I am outside the Eastern Province?

We ship batteries to all Saudi cities through our store, with payment by Tabby, Tamara or Emkan.
