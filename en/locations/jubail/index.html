Batteries for working life in Jubail

Jubail is an industrial city that relies on trucks, equipment and commercial vehicles, so this branch focuses on workshops, contractors and service drivers as well as private cars. From the Al Musanad branch we take orders for car and heavy vehicle batteries and arrange supply by quantity.

What suits Jubail customers

  • Trucks and equipment: Heavy duty batteries for heavy vehicles.
  • Workshops and contractors: Regular supply and quotes by quantity.
  • Private cars: Standard, EFB and AGM for many makes.
  • SHUBA lithium: Exclusive agent in the Eastern Province.
