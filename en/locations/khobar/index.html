Our two Khobar branches: which to choose

If you are in central Khobar or near 30th Street, the Al Thuqba branch is opposite Bin Jumah Markets on the commercial street, convenient when you combine buying a battery with other errands. If you are heading south on King Fahd Road, the South Al Rakah branch (postal code 34226) is closer.

Batteries for Khobar and Dhahran drivers

  • Family cars and sedans: Standard and EFB batteries for daily use.
  • Newer and premium cars: AGM and SHUBA lithium for higher performance needs.
  • Work vehicles: Batteries for trucks and commercial vehicles.
  • Company orders: Supply to workshops and organisations with special quotes.
