What the service includes

  • Digital battery test: We measure the old battery's condition to confirm whether it really needs replacing.
  • Alternator voltage and charging check: We make sure the alternator charges properly so the new battery is not drained.
  • Safe professional fitting: Fitting suited to your battery tray and terminal direction.
  • Terminal cleaning and protection: We clean the terminals and protect them against corrosion so the connection lasts longer.

Payment on fitting: cash or card machine, depending on availability when the technician arrives.

When do you need this service?

  • Slow cranking or no start at all.
  • The battery died and you have no one to jump-start it.
  • Dim dashboard lights or the battery warning light is on.
  • Your battery is more than 3 to 4 years old.
  • You want a replacement without going to a branch.

FAQ

What are the mobile fitting hours?

Saturday to Thursday 7:30 AM to 11:00 PM, and Friday 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM. Outside these hours you can message us on WhatsApp and we will reply when service resumes.

Is the service available outside the Eastern Province?

No, mobile fitting is for the Eastern Province only. For other Saudi cities we ship the battery through the store with payment by Tabby, Tamara or Emkan.

Do you check the alternator?

Yes, we check alternator voltage and charging along with the battery before fitting.

Do you offer car computer programming after a battery change?

We offer testing and professional fitting, but we do not offer car computer programming. If your car requires it, we will tell you before the appointment.

How do I know the right size for my car?

Use the battery finder on our site or send us your car details and we will confirm the right battery before arriving.

How do I pay?

Payment on fitting, by cash or card machine.
