Warranty

We commit to a genuine warranty from one to three years depending on the battery type, and SHUBA lithium batteries carry a 3-year warranty. We explain the warranty period and terms for each battery at purchase, and provide an approved tax invoice.

FAQ

Do you ship to all Saudi cities?

Yes, we ship to all regions of the Kingdom through our store arkan-battery.com.

Is fitting available in other cities?

Mobile fitting is inside the Eastern Province only. Other cities are served by shipping, and you can have the battery fitted at a workshop near you.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes via Tabby, Tamara and Emkan, subject to each platform's terms.

How long is the warranty?

From one to three years depending on battery type, and SHUBA lithium batteries carry a 3-year warranty.

How do I confirm the size before ordering?

Use the battery finder or message us on WhatsApp with your car make, model and year.
