About the company

Arkan Energy: experience since 2017

Arkan Energy Trading was founded in 2017 in Saudi Arabia as a national company specialising in advanced power solutions for vehicles. We are more than a battery supplier: a team dedicated to keeping you moving and your vehicle safe, backed by deep experience and a precise understanding of the Saudi market.

Our visionTo be a leading partner in vehicle power, taking the sector toward new levels of excellence in line with Saudi Vision 2030, as a model for quality, reliability and innovation.
Our missionTo be the right choice for every customer seeking quality and performance, by providing highly efficient products and services and contributing to the national economy through sustainable, advanced power solutions.
